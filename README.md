#  Autonomous EV & Air Taxi Fleet Performance & Energy Optimization (2030–2035)

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on **data-driven performance analysis and energy optimization of autonomous Electric Vehicle (EV) and Air Taxi fleets** for the period **2030–2035**.

The analysis evaluates fleet utilization, energy consumption, operational efficiency, charging requirements, trip performance, and future demand patterns to identify opportunities for improving fleet efficiency and reducing energy usage.

The project demonstrates how **Data Analytics, Forecasting, KPI Analysis, and Data Visualization** can support decision-making in the future mobility and autonomous transportation sector.

---

##  Project Objectives

* Analyze autonomous EV and Air Taxi fleet performance from **2030 to 2035**
* Evaluate **energy consumption and efficiency**
* Identify trends in fleet utilization and operational performance
* Analyze charging and energy demand
* Compare EV and Air Taxi operational metrics
* Forecast future fleet and energy requirements
* Identify opportunities for energy and operational optimization
* Build interactive dashboards for data-driven decision-making

##  Analysis Performed

### 1. Fleet Performance Analysis

Analyzed fleet utilization, trip volume, vehicle availability, and operational performance across the 2030–2035 period.

### 2. Energy Consumption Analysis

Evaluated energy consumption patterns across different fleet types and operating conditions to identify high-consumption areas.

### 3. Efficiency Analysis

Measured energy efficiency using metrics such as energy consumption per trip and energy consumption per distance traveled.

### 4. Charging Demand Analysis

Analyzed charging requirements and projected future charging demand based on fleet utilization and trip growth.

### 5. Trend Analysis

Identified yearly trends in:

* Fleet size
* Number of trips
* Energy consumption
* Fleet utilization
* Charging demand
* Operational efficiency

### 6. Forecasting

Used historical/projected data to estimate future fleet performance and energy requirements through **2035**.
